# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a professional, harassment-free collaboration environment.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique work rather than people,
respect privacy, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, or sexually explicit
conduct.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation
that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the AgenticsWorld organization owners on
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as
reasonably possible.
